Luxury Automotive Manufacturing in India

India has emerged as a strategic manufacturing hub for German luxury automakers, with Mercedes-Benz, BMW, and Audi establishing local assembly plants to avoid prohibitive import duties and offer competitive pricing.

Mercedes-Benz India Manufacturing Plant​

Location: E-3, MIDC Chakan - Phase III, Chakan Industrial Area, Kuruli & Nighoje, Taluka Khed, Pune - 410501, Maharashtra

Key Details:

  • Size: Largest luxury car manufacturing facility in India, covering over 100 acres
  • Established: 2009
  • Annual Capacity: 20,000 units
  • Production Milestone: Over 200,000 cars manufactured since inception
  • Sustainability: Operates 100% on green energy
  • Flexible Assembly Line: Same production line assembles both ICE vehicles and EVs
  • Global Distinction: First country outside Germany to locally produce Mercedes-Maybach sedans/SUVs and EQS electric sedan
  • Local Partner: Force Motors supplies precision-engineered engines and axles from dedicated Chakan facility

BMW India Manufacturing Plant​

Location: BMW Group Plant, Chennai, Tamil Nadu

BMW localizes the X1 and other models at this facility to maintain competitive pricing in the ₹45-50 lakh range.

Audi India Manufacturing Plant​

Location: SAVWIPL (Skoda Auto Volkswagen India) plant, Aurangabad, Maharashtra

Audi assembles the Q3 and other models here, achieving the lowest entry price among German luxury SUVs.

CKD vs CBU: Import Duty Impact​

Understanding the Manufacturing Models​

CKD (Completely Knocked Down):

  • Components imported in parts, assembled locally
  • Import duty: 15%
  • Massive cost savings compared to CBU

CBU (Completely Built Unit):

  • Fully assembled vehicles imported from Europe
  • Import duty: 70% to 110%
  • Reserved for niche/performance models (AMG, G-Wagen, convertibles)

Locally Assembled Models & Current Pricing (2026)​

Mercedes-Benz India Lineup​

Luxury Sedans & Electric Vehicles:

  • A-Class Limousine: ₹46.50 Lakh (entry-level luxury sedan)
  • C-Class: ₹62.00 - ₹70.00 Lakh (executive sedan)
  • E-Class LWB: ₹78.50 - ₹92.50 Lakh (highest-selling model)
  • S-Class: ₹2.20 - ₹2.38 Cr (flagship sedan)
  • EQS 580 4MATIC: ₹1.30 - ₹1.48 Cr (flagship electric sedan)

Luxury SUVs:

  • GLA: ₹51.80 - ₹55.00 Lakh (cheapest Mercedes SUV)
  • GLC: ₹77.00 Lakh+
  • GLE: ₹1.00 - ₹1.17 Cr
  • GLS: ₹1.28 Cr+ (flagship 7-seater)
  • EQS SUV: ₹1.34 - ₹1.48 Cr (flagship electric SUV)

Ultra-Luxury:

  • Mercedes-Maybach S-Class S 580: ₹2.88 Cr+
  • Mercedes-Maybach GLS 600: ₹2.75 Cr+

Mercedes-Benz GLA Variants​

  • GLA 200 (Petrol): ₹51.80 Lakh | 1.3L Turbo, 7-speed DCT
  • GLA 220d 4MATIC (Diesel): ₹53.00 Lakh | 2.0L Diesel, AWD, 8-speed DCT
  • GLA 220d 4MATIC AMG Line: ₹55.00 Lakh | AMG styling, 19-inch alloys

Benefit of Localization: Without CKD assembly, GLA would cost ₹75+ Lakh (110% import tariff applied), pricing it out of compact luxury segment.

Why Audi Q3 is the Most Aspirational Budget Option​

Price Advantage: ₹7-8 Lakh cheaper than BMW X1 and Mercedes GLA

Standard Features:

  • Quattro AWD (BMW X1 and Mercedes GLA base models are FWD)
  • 2.0L turbocharged petrol engine (188 bhp)
  • Premium badge at lowest cost

Alternative: Volkswagen Tiguan R-Line (₹45.73 Lakh) offers same platform/engine as Audi with VW badge but superior specs: 201 bhp, 4MOTION AWD, Level 2 ADAS, matrix LED headlights.

Key Takeaways​

  1. Local assembly reduces luxury car prices by ₹15-60 Lakh depending on segment
  2. All three German brands (Mercedes, BMW, Audi) assemble entry-level models locally to avoid 70-110% CBU import duty
  3. Audi Q3 is the cheapest German luxury SUV at ₹43.67 Lakh with standard AWD
  4. Mercedes-Benz GLA benefits most from localization - would cost ₹75+ Lakh if fully imported
  5. India is the only country outside Germany to locally produce Mercedes-Maybach and EQS electric models
