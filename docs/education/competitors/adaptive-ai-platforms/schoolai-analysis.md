SchoolAI is an AI-native educational platform designed to help teachers personalize learning and monitor student progress in real-time, with the tagline "AI Built for Education."

## Company Overview

**Core Value Proposition:** "AI that connects teachers with every student" - helps teachers "see how every student is doing, know what to do next, and spend more time with the students who need you most."

**Target Audience:**
- Teachers (primary users who design learning experiences)
- School Leaders/Administrators
- Students (K-12 and Higher Education)
- Higher Education institutions

## Core Products & Features

### 1. Spaces

SchoolAI Spaces allow teachers to design powerful, tailored learning experiences for every student. Teachers create customized AI-powered learning environments where students interact with the AI tutor named "Dot."

**Key Characteristics:**
- Designed by teachers for their specific classroom needs
- AI personalizes content based on student interests
- 23,000+ Spaces created by educators
- ~60% designed for reasoning, not recall (analysis of 23K+ Spaces)

### 2. Mission Control

Real-time student monitoring dashboard that shows teachers "exactly who's engaged, who's stuck, and what to do next, so no one slips through the cracks."

**Features:**
- Real-time student progress tracking
- Identifies struggling students immediately
- Provides actionable insights for teacher intervention
- Prevents students from falling behind

### 3. PowerUps

Focused AI apps that let students create, practice, and explore for more engaging learning experiences.

**Capabilities:**
- Interactive creation tools
- Practice exercises
- Exploratory learning activities

### 4. Browser Extension

Integrates AI-powered feedback, lesson creation, and personalized learning experiences directly into existing educational tools.

**Benefits:**
- Works within tools teachers already use
- Reduces workflow disruption
- Seamless integration with existing edtech stack

### 5. Professional Learning

Teacher training and development support to maximize platform effectiveness.

## How It Works

1. **Design:** Teachers create learning experiences called "Spaces"
2. **Interact:** Students engage with AI tutor "Dot" within these Spaces
3. **Personalize:** AI adapts content based on student interests and needs
4. **Monitor:** Mission Control dashboard shows real-time student progress
5. **Intervene:** Teachers provide targeted support where AI identifies need

## Evidence & Research

**ESSA Level III Certification (Promising Evidence):**
- Third-party validated effectiveness
- Meets federal standards for evidence-based interventions

**Jordan School District Study:**
- **Participants:** 82 teachers, 13,882 AI conversations
- **Results:** 28% boost in student critical thinking
- **Methodology:** Independent third-party evaluation

**Platform Analytics:**
- ~60% of 23,000+ teacher-designed Spaces focus on reasoning over recall
- Demonstrates pedagogical shift toward higher-order thinking

## Safety & Compliance

**Data Privacy & Security:**
- FERPA certified (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act)
- COPPA certified (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- AICPA SOC 2 compliant
- 1EdTech TrustEd Apps Certified
- FIPPA certified

**AI Safety Features:**
- Flags crises in real time
- Never poses as human
- Contractually bound never to train on student data
- Contractually bound never to sell student data

## Pricing Model

**Freemium Structure:**
- Free tier available for individual teachers
- District-wide demos available for institutional adoption
- Pricing details not publicly disclosed (contact for enterprise pricing)

## Competitive Positioning

**Strengths:**
1. **Teacher-Centric Design:** Platform designed FOR teachers to control, not AI replacing teachers
2. **Evidence-Based:** ESSA Level III certification rare among AI education tools
3. **Compliance-First:** Comprehensive privacy/security certifications
4. **Real-Time Insights:** Mission Control provides immediate intervention signals
5. **Flexible Integration:** Browser extension works with existing tools

**Market Position:**
- Positioned between fully automated AI tutors (like Synthesis Tutor, ASI) and traditional learning management systems
- Focus on "AI-augmented teaching" rather than "AI replacement teaching"
- Targets K-12 and higher education (broader than test-prep focused platforms)
