**VTWAX** (Vanguard Total World Stock Index Fund, Admiral Shares) is a US mutual fund that holds essentially every publicly investable stock in the world — developed and emerging markets, large through small cap — in a single ticker. It is the mutual-fund share class of the same underlying fund whose ETF share class trades as [VT](economics/investment-products/equity-funds/international-etfs.md).

## Share Classes of the Same Fund

VTWAX, its Investor Shares counterpart, and the VT ETF all track the same FTSE Global All Cap Index and hold the same underlying portfolio — they differ only in structure, minimums, and cost:

| Feature | VTWAX (Admiral) | VTWSX (Investor) | VT (ETF) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Structure** | Mutual fund | Mutual fund | ETF |
| **Expense Ratio** | 0.09% | 0.16% | 0.06% |
| **Minimum Investment** | $3,000 | $3,000 | 1 share (~market price) |
| **Trading** | Once daily at NAV | Once daily at NAV | Intraday, like a stock |
| **Auto-invest / fractional SIPs** | Yes | Yes | Limited by broker |

**Practical implication:** if a brokerage supports fractional/automatic recurring investments into mutual funds, VTWAX is a convenient "set and forget" single-fund holding. If not (or for non-US brokerage access, e.g. via IBKR), the ETF share class [VT](economics/investment-products/equity-funds/international-etfs.md) is the equivalent, more universally tradeable instrument — and for Indian investors specifically, the Ireland-domiciled [VWRA](economics/investment-products/equity-funds/international-etfs.md) (tracking the related FTSE All-World Index) is often preferred over VT/VTWAX for estate-tax reasons. See [US Estate Tax Avoidance for Non-Resident Aliens](economics/taxation/us-estate-tax-avoidance-nra.md).

## One-Fund Portfolio

VTWAX is a common pick for a **one-fund equity portfolio** in the Bogleheads community — since it already blends total US and total international stock market exposure in a single fund, it removes the need to separately hold and rebalance a US fund (e.g. VTSAX) against an international fund (e.g. VTIAX). This is the simplest variant of the [Three-fund portfolio](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Three-fund_portfolio) philosophy, collapsed into one ticker for the equity sleeve.
