## Summary

Hero MotoCorp and Ather Energy sit at opposite ends of the value/growth spectrum within the Indian two-wheeler industry. Hero is a profitable, near debt-free, cash-generative market leader trading at a discount to peers; Ather is a fast-improving but still loss-making EV pure-play trading at a steep growth premium.
