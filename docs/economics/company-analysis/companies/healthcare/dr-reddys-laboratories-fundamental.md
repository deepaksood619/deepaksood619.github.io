## Business Overview

**Company Profile:**

Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd is a major Indian pharmaceutical company with a globally diversified footprint across generics, active pharmaceutical ingredients, biosimilars, and — since a 2024 acquisition — consumer healthcare.

**Business Model:**

- **Global Generics (~89% of revenue):** North America (largest single market, pressured by Lenalidomide/Revlimid generic volume normalization and US price erosion, -7% YoY in Q1 FY27), Europe (+13% YoY, boosted by the acquired NRT consumer brands), India (+17% YoY), Emerging Markets (+31% YoY — the standout growth segment).
- **PSAI (Pharmaceutical Services & Active Ingredients / API) (~11% of revenue):** +8.4% YoY in Q1 FY27, a steadier base-load business.
- **Biosimilars:** Denosumab launched in EU; Rituximab US approval pending inspection clearance; an IV formulation BLA under FDA review, approval expected late CY2026.
- **Consumer Healthcare:** Built around the 2024 Haleon NRT (nicotine replacement therapy) brand acquisition outside the US — Nicotinell, Nicabate (Australia), Thrive (Canada), Habitrol (Canada/NZ) — ~95% integration complete as of FY26.

**Semaglutide/GLP-1 — major FY27 development:** Dr. Reddy's was the first Indian company to receive DCGI approval for generic injectable semaglutide, launched as **Obeda** in India (~Rs. 4,200/month) following the March 2026 patent expiry, targeting ~12 million pens/year across markets in year one. However, out-of-specification batches traced to a synthetic API impurity forced a **Rs. 240 Cr provision** in Q1 FY27 and loss of PLI benefits, with commercial resupply targeted for **November 2026** (6-7 million pens expected across Q3-Q4 FY27).

**Market Position:**

- Major player in US generics, particularly complex/injectable generics and the Lenalidomide (Revlimid generic) franchise
- Emerging leader in biosimilars among Indian pharma companies
- Key competitors: Sun Pharma, Cipla, Lupin (domestic and global generics); Teva, Viatris (US generics); Sandoz, Biocon (biosimilars)

**Competitive Moat:**

- Diversified geographic mix reduces single-market dependency (India +17%, Emerging Markets +31% partly offsetting NA weakness)
- Early-mover position in Indian GLP-1 generics (Obeda brand), despite the Q1 FY27 execution stumble
- Growing biosimilars pipeline provides a differentiated, higher-barrier growth avenue beyond small-molecule generics

**Management Quality:**

- **Leadership:** Erez Israeli (CEO since 2019, 23 years at Teva Pharmaceuticals prior); G.V. Prasad (Co-Chairman & Managing Director); K. Satish Reddy (Chairman)
- **Track Record:** Built one of India's most globally diversified generics businesses; pivoted early into biosimilars and consumer healthcare (Haleon NRT deal) to reduce pure-generics dependency
- **Concerns:** The semaglutide API quality failure and subsequent Pomerantz securities-fraud investigation are significant, recent execution/governance red flags requiring close monitoring

**Corporate Governance:**

- **Promoter Holding:** 26.63% (declared **zero pledge/encumbrance** for FY26)

- **Institutional Support:** FII 20.67% (down from 25.75% Mar 2025), DII 31.75% (up sharply from 25.63% Mar 2025) — DII accumulation is a notable positive offsetting FII selling
