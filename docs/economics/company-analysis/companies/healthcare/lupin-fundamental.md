## Business Overview

**Company Profile:**

Lupin Limited, founded by Dr. Desh Bandhu Gupta and headquartered in Mumbai, is an "innovation-led transnational pharmaceutical company" operating across generics, biosimilars, specialty brands, and APIs in 100+ countries. It is the 3rd largest player in the US by prescriptions (generics + total Rx) and the 8th largest company in the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM), with global leadership positions in anti-TB and cephalosporin segments and strength in cardiovascular, diabetology, respiratory/asthma, pediatrics, and CNS therapies.

**Business Model (FY26 revenue mix, Rs. Cr):**

Q1 FY27 (June 2026 quarter) showed the same mix accelerating further: US +42.9% YoY, India +13.9% YoY, Other Developed Markets +48.3% YoY, Emerging Markets +51.7% YoY, API +8.5% YoY — total revenue +33.3% YoY.

**US pipeline detail:** 3rd largest generics player in the US by prescriptions; 149-151 generic products marketed; market leader in 56-61 marketed generics and top-3 in 112 products; 344-350 cumulative ANDA approvals out of 429-430 filed; 50-52 First-to-File filings (21-22 exclusive). Q1 FY27 alone saw 6 ANDA approvals and 3 new US launches.

**Respiratory franchise:** gSpiriva (tiotropium, FDA-approved 2023 — the first US generic of Boehringer Ingelheim's Spiriva HandiHaler), complex inhalation products mirroring Advair (fluticasone-salmeterol) and Pulmicort (budesonide), targeting the global inhaler market projected at `>`$45 Bn by 2027. In India: Budamate, Telekast, AB-Flo, Difizma (India's first Ultra LABA+LAMA+ICS), and Vilfuro-G (world's first fixed-dose triple DPI combination for COPD). Respiratory and diabetes therapies now `>`28% of branded-generics sales in regulated markets, up from `<`20% in FY21.

**Biosimilars/specialty pivot:** Lupin sold its US commercial women's-health specialty business (including Solosec) to Evofem Biosciences in July 2024 for up to $84 Mn in milestones, explicitly to refocus specialty efforts on respiratory and neurology. It then signed a global biosimilar Ranibizumab (anti-VEGF, wet AMD/DME) partnership with Sandoz (August 2025, covering EU ex-Germany, Switzerland, Norway, Australia, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Malaysia, with Lupin retaining manufacturing/regulatory filings) and an exclusive US licensing deal with Valorum Biologics (December 2025) for biosimilar pegfilgrastim ("Armlupeg"), under which Valorum commercializes in the US while Lupin manufactures/supplies and receives upfront fees plus royalties.

**Market Position:**

- 3rd largest US generics player by prescriptions; 8th largest player in the Indian Pharmaceutical Market
- Global leader in anti-TB and cephalosporin API/formulation segments
- Key competitors: Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's, Cipla, Aurobindo (US generics); Teva, Viatris (global generics); Sandoz, Biocon (biosimilars)

**Competitive Moat:**

- One of the largest ANDA pipelines among Indian pharma peers (429-430 filed, 344-350 approved), providing a broad, continuously-refreshing US launch cadence
- Differentiated respiratory/complex-inhalation manufacturing capability (gSpiriva as the first-to-market complex generic of its kind) — a genuine technical barrier versus simple oral-solid generics
- Strategic discipline in divesting non-core specialty assets (women's health) to concentrate R&D/manufacturing synergies in respiratory and biosimilars

**Management Quality:**

- **Leadership:** Vinita D. Gupta (CEO since September 2013, reappointed May 28, 2025); Nilesh Gupta (Managing Director since September 2023) — both children of founder Dr. Desh Bandhu Gupta; Ramesh Swaminathan (Executive Director, Global CFO & Head of API Plus SBU, reappointed March 26, 2025)
- **Track Record:** Successfully navigated Lupin from a steep FY22 loss year (US pricing pressure/one-off charges, EPS -Rs. 33.62) to sustained double-digit growth and margin expansion over the following four years — a genuine turnaround
- **Concerns:** Recent USFDA Form 483 observations across multiple facilities (see Risk Assessment) require continued management attention to compliance

1. US momentum sustains as the complex-generics/respiratory mix continues expanding EBITDA margins toward global-peer levels
2. India business re-acceleration (already +13.9-15.1% YoY in Q1 FY27) continues on new brand launches and chronic-therapy focus

4. Valuation re-rates back toward peer multiples (25-30x) as the market recognizes the disconnect between Lupin's superior growth/returns and its currently depressed PE

### Bear Case (Target: Rs. 1,750 — ~-17% downside)

1. UBS's GLP-1 competitive-risk thesis proves correct — US generic GLP-1 pricing collapses faster than expected as multiple players enter, eroding a key growth vector
2. US Section 232-style tariffs on generic drug imports move from the current 0% (through 2028) toward escalation sooner than the stated phased timeline, given India's ~42% US revenue exposure
3. A USFDA Form 483 observation (e.g., at Goa, which had the highest count at 7) escalates to a formal warning letter or import alert
4. Margin expansion normalizes/reverses as the current elevated 29-33% OPM level was partly driven by a favorable, potentially temporary, US product mix

### Base Case (Target: Rs. 2,500)

Growth continues at a more moderate but still healthy pace (India settles into low-teens growth, US growth moderates from the +42.9% Q1 FY27 pace but remains double-digit); the stock re-rates partially toward peer multiples as consensus estimates (Rs. ~2,509 average across 36 analysts) are met.

## Conclusion

Lupin presents the clearest valuation anomaly in this sector coverage: a business posting 16 consecutive quarters of YoY growth, the best ROE (28.7%) and ROCE (~30%) among large-cap Indian pharma peers, and a free cash flow profile that nearly quadrupled in FY26 — yet trading at roughly half the PE multiple of Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's, or Cipla following a sentiment-driven pullback (a UBS sector downgrade on GLP-1 competitive-risk grounds, plus the July 2026 US generic-tariff announcement). The underlying risks (GLP-1 pricing competition, long-dated tariff escalation, recurring but so-far-contained FDA facility observations) are real and worth monitoring, but none of them show up yet in the actual financial results, which remain the strongest in the sector. This combination of quality, growth, and a genuine valuation discount makes Lupin the highest-conviction Buy among the companies covered in this sector report.
