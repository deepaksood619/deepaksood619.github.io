## Business Overview

**Company Profile:**

Divi's Laboratories, founded in 1990 by Dr. Murali K. Divi, manufactures and exports Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), pharma intermediates, and nutraceutical ingredients (carotenoids). It is India's largest API/CRAMS player and a top-2 global producer for 18 of the 32 APIs it manufactures.

**Business Model:**

- **Generic APIs (~45-55% of revenue):** World's largest manufacturer for several generic APIs.
- **Custom Synthesis/CRAMS (~35-45% of revenue, rising sharply — ~55-60% in recent quarters):** Contract manufacturing for innovator/Big Pharma clients, including Novartis, Sanofi, GSK, and Merck among named relationships over the past decade. This segment's rising share reflects new capex-project ramp-ups (validation batches for three major customer-backed projects).
- **Nutraceuticals (~10-15% of revenue):** Carotenoid ingredients for food/dietary-supplement/feed makers.
- Exports ~85-87% of revenue — a large forex exposure, largely USD-denominated.

**Capacity expansion — Kakinada SEZ Unit 3:** Began operations January 2025 as a backward-integration hub; six process blocks operational, two more under construction during FY26. FY26 capex guidance was raised to ~Rs. 2,000 Cr (actual spend ~Rs. 1,500 Cr), aimed at peptide/custom-synthesis capacity expected to commercialize over a 12-24 month horizon.

**Market Position:**

- India's largest API/CDMO company by scale and profitability
- Serves 12 of the top 20 global Big Pharma companies over the last 10 years
- Key competitors: other Indian API/CDMO players (smaller scale), Chinese API manufacturers (aggressive on price)

**Competitive Moat:**

- Scale and regulatory track record in generic APIs and custom synthesis — a top-2 global producer for the majority of its API portfolio
- Deep, multi-year relationships with global innovator pharma clients, positioning it as a "natural partner" for GLP-1/peptide supply-chain scaling as patents expire
- Debt-free balance sheet with large net cash provides capacity to fund capex-intensive growth without dilution or leverage risk

**Management Quality:**

- **Leadership:** Dr. Murali K. Divi (Founder, Managing Director since 1990) — FY26 highest-paid pharma executive in India (total remuneration Rs. 100.26 Cr); Dr. Kiran S. Divi (Whole-time Director & CEO since 2020); Dr. Ramesh B.V. Nimmagadda (Chairman)
- **Track Record:** Built Divi's from inception into India's largest and most profitable API company over three decades
- **Concerns:** High promoter compensation relative to peers; no other major governance concerns identified

**Corporate Governance:**

- **Promoter Holding:** 51.88% (stable)
- **Promoter Pledge:** 0% — confirmed unpledged
- **Institutional Support:** FII 20.19%, DII 19.29% — balanced institutional participation
