## Business Overview

**Company Profile:**

Cipla Ltd, controlled by the Hamied family, is one of India's oldest and most respected pharmaceutical companies, historically known for its respiratory portfolio and role in making HIV/AIDS drugs affordable globally.

**Business Model:**

- **One India (~45% of revenue, Rs. `>`12,680 Cr FY26):** Branded prescription, trade generics, and consumer health. Chronic therapies (respiratory, cardiac, anti-diabetes, urology) now `>`60% of the branded prescription mix. Foracort (inhaler) crossed Rs. 1,000 Cr in annual brand revenue — India's #1 respiratory brand. Cipla ranks 3rd largest pharma company in India by revenue.
- **North America (~22-24% of revenue, ~$780 Mn FY26, -13% YoY):** Decline driven by loss of gRevlimid exclusivity revenue and the Lanreotide supply disruption. Albuterol holds ~22% US market share; gAdvair (generic Advair Diskus) is a pending, late-entrant launch.
- **One Africa/SAGA (~15-19% of revenue, ~$483 Mn FY26, +7% YoY):** South Africa tender market +13.6% YoY to $92 Mn.
- **EMEU/Emerging Markets & Europe (~12-13% of revenue).**
- **API & Others (~1% of revenue).**

**Market Position:**

- #1 respiratory brand in India (Foracort); 3rd largest respiratory player by prescription in the US
- 3rd largest pharma company in India by revenue
- Key competitors: Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's, Lupin (domestic/global generics); Teva, Mylan, Hikma, Aurobindo (respiratory generics — gAdvair race)

**Competitive Moat:**

- Strong India respiratory brand equity (Foracort) built over decades, difficult to displace
- Debt-free balance sheet provides financial flexibility
- Differentiated respiratory devices (e.g., Synchrobreathe inhaler) have secured NPPA pricing above the standard ceiling — a rare pricing-power exception in a price-controlled market

**Management Quality:**

- **Leadership transition:** Umang Vohra (MD & Global CEO since 2016) stepped down effective March 31, 2026; **Achin Gupta** (previously CEO of One India business, then Global COO) became MD & Global CEO effective April 1, 2026 for a 5-year term
- **Track Record:** Vohra-era decade built the "One India" consolidation strategy and diversified geographic mix; Gupta's internal promotion (from running the India business) signals continuity of strategic focus
- **Concerns:** Any new-CEO transition carries execution continuity risk during a period of margin pressure

**Corporate Governance:**

- **Promoter Holding:** 29.22% (down from ~33.40% Mar 2024, mainly due to a 2.53% stake sale worth ~Rs. 2,637 Cr in May 2024 by Hamied family members explicitly to fund philanthropic activities — not a distress sale)
- **Promoter Pledge:** 0% — promoters formally declared zero shares encumbered for FY26
- **Institutional Support:** FII 20.20%, DII 34.35% — the strongest DII backing among the four large-cap pharma majors covered in this sector report

## Conclusion

Cipla remains one of the higher-quality names in Indian pharma — debt-free, India's #1 respiratory brand, and backed by the strongest DII confidence in the sector (34.35%) — but FY26/Q1 FY27 results expose a real vulnerability: dependence on third-party CDMOs (the Pharmathen/Lanreotide halt) and the fading of unusually high-margin, non-repeatable revenue streams (gRevlimid). The stock's ~11.6% one-year decline appears to already price in a meaningful amount of this bad news, and the debt-free balance sheet provides a cushion while margins normalize. This is a name to accumulate on weakness rather than chase, pending confirmation that FY27 margin guidance is being met.
