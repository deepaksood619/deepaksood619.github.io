**Company Profile:**

Biocon Limited was founded in 1978 by Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw in Bangalore, India. Starting as a small enzyme manufacturing company, Biocon has transformed into India's leading biopharmaceutical company with global operations across biosimilars, generics, and active pharmaceutical ingredients.
