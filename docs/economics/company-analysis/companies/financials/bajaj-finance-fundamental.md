## Business Overview

**Company Profile:**
Bajaj Finance Limited (BFL) is headquartered in Pune and is part of the Bajaj Group. It is mainly engaged in lending — a diversified portfolio across retail, SME and commercial customers with significant presence across urban and rural India — and also accepts public/corporate deposits and cross-sells insurance and payments products.

**Business Model:**

- **Revenue Streams:** Net interest income (spread between lending yield and cost of borrowings) plus fee and other income from a diversified loan book (consumer durables, personal loans, two/three-wheeler finance, SME loans, commercial lending, mortgages via Bajaj Housing Finance).
- **Key Products/Services:** Consumer durable/personal/two-wheeler loans, SME and commercial lending, co-branded credit cards (Insta EMI Card), digital app/EMI network, fixed deposits.
- **Distribution:** Pan-India branch and merchant-partner network combined with a large digital/app-based EMI ecosystem.

**Market Position:**

- **Market Rank:** India's largest private-sector diversified NBFC by market cap and scale.
- **Key Competitors:** Cholamandalam Investment & Finance, Shriram Finance, L&T Finance, HDB Financial Services, and consumer-lending arms of large private banks.

**Competitive Moat:**

- Scale + data-driven underwriting built over a large multi-decade customer franchise across retail and SME segments.
- Distribution reach via merchant tie-ups (consumer durables) and the wider Bajaj Group ecosystem (cross-sell with Bajaj Finserv's insurance arms).
- Diversified lending book (retail + SME + commercial) reduces single-segment concentration risk relative to mono-line NBFCs.

**Management Quality:**

- **Leadership:** Rajeev Jain (MD), under the ownership of Bajaj Finserv/Bajaj Group promoter entities.
- **Track Record:** Sustained multi-year compounding of profit (34.2% 5-year CAGR per Screener.in) through multiple credit cycles.
- **Concerns:** The RBI has in the past taken supervisory action against specific digital-lending products at BFL over compliance/disclosure gaps (temporary restrictions on select loan-disbursal products, later lifted) — noted here as a historical episode reflecting regulatory scrutiny risk typical of large digital lenders; verify current status directly with the company/RBI before acting on this point, as it was not independently re-confirmed in this session's data pull.

**Corporate Governance:**

- **Promoter Holding:** 54.68% (Jun 2026) — adequate, though down from 55.87% (Sep 2023), largely due to a step-down between Sep 2023 and Mar 2024; broadly stable since (54.69-54.73% band).
- **Promoter Pledge:** No pledge reported on the fetched shareholding data — clean.
- **Institutional Support:** FII 20.20% (Jun 2026, down from 21.33% in Mar 2026), DII 16.30% (Jun 2026, up from 15.10% in Mar 2026) — DII buying has offset FII selling in the latest quarter.

## Conclusion

Bajaj Finance is unambiguously a high-quality franchise: India's largest private NBFC, compounding profit at ~29-34% over multiple years, with a diversified book, zero promoter pledge, and asset quality that — while ticking up modestly — remains strong in absolute terms. The complication is valuation: at 34.9x PE and 6.28x book value, the stock is priced richer than every large diversified NBFC peer examined here, including Cholamandalam, which currently offers a comparable-to-better ROE at a meaningfully cheaper multiple.

This is a "great business, full price" situation rather than an obvious mispricing in either direction. Existing holders have limited reason to sell given the underlying compounding, but new capital is better deployed on a pullback toward the Rs. 950-1,050 zone (closer to peer-average multiples) unless growth/asset-quality trends improve enough to justify sustaining the current premium.

**Investment Recommendation:** Hold (existing positions) / Accumulate on dips toward Rs. 950-1,050 (new capital)
**Suitable For:** Long-term investors seeking exposure to India's formal consumer/SME credit growth story, who are comfortable paying a quality premium
**Portfolio Allocation:** 3-6% of portfolio (large-cap core financial holding, not a concentrated bet)
