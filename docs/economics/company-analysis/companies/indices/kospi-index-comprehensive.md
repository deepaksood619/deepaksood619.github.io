## Index Overview

**What KOSPI Is:**
KOSPI (Korea Composite Stock Price Index) tracks all common stocks listed on the Korea Exchange (KRX) main board, weighted by market capitalization. It is the primary benchmark for South Korean equities, alongside the narrower, tradable KOSPI 200.

**Composition & Concentration (Key Structural Fact):**

- Samsung Electronics: ~26.04% of index market cap
- SK Hynix: ~20.90% of index market cap
- **Combined: ~46.94%** — the highest concentration level in 16 months, and for the first time ever the two names exceeded 50% combined weight within the narrower KOSPI 200 (May 2026)
- This concentration rose sharply through 2026 as the AI/HBM memory boom accelerated (from 22.63% at end of 2025)
- Only 2 of 26 KOSPI sectors — semiconductors (+38.6%) and automobiles (+29.1%) — outperformed the index's already-strong gains in a mid-May 2026 window, confirming the rally (and the current unwind) is semiconductor-led, not broad-based

**Market Position:**

- Primary gauge of South Korean equity market health; heavily influenced by global semiconductor/AI capex cycles and USD/KRW dynamics
- Increasingly correlated with US tech/Nasdaq moves given Samsung/SK Hynix's role in the global memory supply chain (HBM for AI servers)
