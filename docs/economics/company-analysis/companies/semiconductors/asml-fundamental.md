## Business Overview

**Company Profile:**
ASML Holding N.V., headquartered in Veldhoven, the Netherlands, was founded in 1984 as a joint venture between Philips and ASM International. It designs, develops, and manufactures photolithography systems used by semiconductor manufacturers to print circuit patterns on silicon wafers.

**Business Model:**

- **Revenue Streams:** New systems sales (DUV and EUV lithography machines) and Installed Base Management (servicing, upgrades, refurbishment of the ~5,000+ machine installed base) — the latter is a high-margin, recurring revenue stream that grew to €2.8B in Q2 2026 alone.
- **Key Products/Services:** Deep Ultraviolet (DUV) immersion lithography systems; Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography systems (Low-NA and next-generation High-NA EUV).
- **Distribution:** Direct sales relationship with a small number of the world's largest chipmakers; systems cost $150M-$400M+ each and require years of lead time.

**Market Position:**

- **Market Rank:** #1 — sole global supplier of EUV lithography systems; among the top 2-3 suppliers of DUV lithography alongside Nikon and Canon (neither of which has commercialized EUV).
- **Key Competitors:** Nikon, Canon (DUV only — no EUV capability); no direct competitor exists in EUV.

**Competitive Moat:**

- **Technology monopoly:** Sole commercial supplier of EUV lithography, the only technology capable of printing sub-7nm circuit patterns economically.
- **Ecosystem lock-in:** Exclusive optics partnership with Zeiss and proprietary light-source technology (from the 2013 Cymer acquisition) — decades of accumulated IP that competitors cannot replicate.
- **Extreme switching costs:** Fabs are physically and operationally designed around ASML's tools; qualifying an alternative supplier for leading-edge nodes is not commercially viable.
- **High-NA EUV lead:** Intel has already begun using High-NA EUV tools in production — the next technology generation is also ASML-exclusive.

**Management Quality:**

- **Leadership:** Christophe Fouquet became CEO in 2024, succeeding long-tenured Peter Wennink; deep internal succession from an engineering-led culture.
- **Track Record:** Consistent multi-decade execution on one of the most technically demanding manufacturing processes in the world; guidance raised twice during 2026.
- **Concerns:** None significant identified from available public data.

**Corporate Governance:**

- **Ownership Structure:** ASML is a widely-held Dutch public company with no controlling promoter/founder — differs structurally from the Indian promoter-holding model. Institutional ownership: 50.57%. Insider ownership: 0.01% (typical and not a red flag for a company of this profile and market).
- **Analyst Confidence:** "Strong Buy" consensus from 44 analysts (per S&P Global data), average target ~$2,117.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (Target: ~$2,300+ — ~40%+ upside)

1. AI and HBM/DRAM memory capex supercycle continues to broaden and deepen through 2027-2028, with order visibility already extending that far.
2. High-NA EUV adoption accelerates beyond Intel to other logic and memory customers faster than expected, opening a new high-margin systems category.
3. China export restrictions remain contained to the lower-margin DUV product line (EUV has never shipped there), limiting the earnings impact even as headlines suggest broader risk.

### Bear Case (Target: ~$950-1,100 — ~35-40% downside)

1. Semiconductor capital equipment is a historically cyclical industry (see the 2023 FCF trough); a post-2026 capex digestion phase among logic/memory customers could sharply slow bookings.
2. Escalating geopolitical restrictions (e.g., the proposed MATCH Act) extend beyond DUV to threaten a larger share of revenue, or China's nascent domestic immersion DUV production (5 units planned in 2026, 20 planned for the following year) erodes the low end of the addressable market faster than expected.
3. Valuation multiple compresses from the current 52x trailing PE back toward the historical ~30x average as growth normalizes, independent of any fundamental deterioration.

### Base Case (Target: ~$1,900-2,050)

FY2026 guidance (€43-45B revenue, 54-56% gross margin) is delivered largely as guided, High-NA EUV ramps gradually through 2027, and the valuation multiple moderates modestly while remaining at a structural premium to the broader semicap peer group given the durability of the EUV moat.

## Conclusion

ASML sits at the single most defensible chokepoint in the global semiconductor supply chain: it is the only company on earth that can sell EUV lithography systems, and it is entering a multi-year window where AI and memory capex are pushing its largest customers to spend at record levels. Financially, the business is exceptional — expanding margins, ~54% ROE, a net-cash balance sheet, and free cash flow that has nearly tripled off its 2023 cyclical trough. The counterweight is valuation: at a 52x trailing PE that sits 71% above its own historical average, after a 131%+ 12-month rally, the stock is pricing in a great deal of the good news already, and customer concentration plus geopolitical export-control risk are real, non-trivial exposures rather than academic tail risks.

For existing holders, this supports a hold with conviction — the moat and growth outlook are intact and nothing here argues for exiting a structurally superior business. For new capital, the better risk/reward is to build a position gradually and reserve the bulk of sizing for pullbacks toward the $1,300-1,600 zone rather than adding aggressively at all-time-high valuations.
