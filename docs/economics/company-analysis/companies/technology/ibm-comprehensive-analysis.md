## Business Overview

IBM is a 113-year-old technology and consulting company (founded 1911) with 264,300 employees operating globally across Americas, Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Asia Pacific. The company has transformed from a hardware-centric mainframe provider to a **hybrid cloud and AI platform company** following strategic divestitures (Kyndryl infrastructure spin-off in 2021) and acquisitions (Red Hat for $34B in 2019).

**Core Value Proposition:** Helping enterprises navigate digital and AI transformations across applications, data, and hybrid/multi-cloud environments.
