Isabgol, also known as psyllium husk, is a natural soluble fiber derived from the seeds of the Plantago ovata plant. When mixed with liquid, it absorbs water and forms a gel-like substance that adds bulk to stool, making it a highly effective and popular remedy for managing constipation, diarrhea, and overall gut health.

## Key Health Benefits

**Constipation Relief**

It acts as a bulk-forming laxative. The gel adds moisture and volume to stool, promoting regular and comfortable bowel movements.

**Diarrhea Management**

It helps absorb excess water in the gut, which can slow down colon transit and firm up loose stools.

**Weight Management**

By expanding in the stomach, it promotes a feeling of fullness, which can help curb overeating and manage food cravings.

**Heart and Metabolic Health**

Regular intake can assist in lowering cholesterol levels and regulating blood sugar.

## How to Consume It

**For Constipation**

Mix 1–2 teaspoons in a full glass of warm water or milk and consume immediately before bedtime.

**For Diarrhea/Gut Health**

Mix 1–2 teaspoons with curd (yogurt) and eat shortly after a meal.

**For Acidity**

Combine it with cold milk to soothe the stomach lining.

## Critical Precautions

**Drink Plenty of Water**

Always consume isabgol with a generous amount of water or other fluids. Failing to drink enough water can cause the fiber to swell and potentially lead to throat or bowel blockages.

**Medication Spacing**

Because it slows digestion and absorption, you should take prescribed medications at least 1–2 hours before or after consuming isabgol to prevent interference.

**Long-term Use**

Long-term, daily consumption of high-fiber supplements can slightly reduce the absorption of certain minerals (like iron, zinc, and calcium).
