Notes from a Hindi documentary by Abhinav Pande (News Pinch, 2026) that leans toward the government narrative and features officials and former cadres. Figures come from an auto-generated transcript and some are uncertain. Interviewee and creator claims are labelled as such.

## Timeline

- **1967:** the Naxalbari uprising in West Bengal; Charu Majumdar was the ideologue of armed struggle, with Kanu Sanyal and Jangal Santhal
- **1980:** Kondapalli Seetharamaiah's group formed People's War in North Telangana and chose Dandakaranya (Bastar) as its rear base
- **Bastar:** a single district until 1998 and roughly the size of Kerala; Chhattisgarh was formed in November 2000 with about 20-22k police
- **1993:** Tarem blast (11-12 killed)
- **2005:** Salwa Judum began (about June); in 2011 the Supreme Court ruled against arming civilians as Special Police Officers
- **2010:** Tadmetla ambush (76 CRPF personnel killed)
- **2013:** Jhiram Ghati attack (25 May) killed Congress leaders
- **2025:** Maoist Central Committee chief Basavaraju was killed in May with his protection company, and 18 Maoists were killed in an encounter on 3 Dec
- **2026:** Home Minister Amit Shah's deadline of 31 March for ending Left Wing Extremism; PIB/MHA figures quote 126 affected districts at the peak, down to 2 by March 2026, and the documentary states the deadline was met

## Structure and Funding

- Central Committee and Politburo, then special zonal committees (Dandakaranya, AOB, North Telangana, MMC, Western Ghats), then divisional and area committees
- PLGA Battalion No. 1 was the only full battalion (formed 2009 per the video)
- **Janatana Sarkar** (revolutionary people's committees) covered 3-4 villages with 8 branches, a militia, jan adalat (people's courts) and levies
- **Funding:** the tendu leaf levy was a main source (one area committee reportedly took ₹30 lakh a year, up to ₹1 crore by 2019, per an ex-cadre; uncertain), plus a ₹10 party fee per member

## Government Response (Claimed)

- DRG (District Reserve Guard, a local force) described as the game changer; Operation Kagar
- The Niyad Nella Naar scheme (50+ villages), 3,000+ surrenders and support payments to surrendered cadres, and nationalised tendu leaf buying (all as stated by the government side; amounts uncertain)

## Claims by Interviewees and the Creator

- The creator says Maoism caused more harm than terrorism, and that adivasis were "caught between two guns"; he shows Hidma's village, Puvarti (Sukma), now with a school, anganwadi and road
- Former cadres say they joined because of forest-guard harassment and poverty, and that 99% of Maoist automatic weapons were looted from security forces (rebutting a foreign-supply theory)
- Locals allege fake encounters and arbitrary arrests of villagers, and fear that mining and corporate projects will follow ("only Naxalism saved the forest"); officials call this an opposition narrative
- Closing commentary: tribal fears over land and forest after Naxalism ends
