Dehradun experiences Moderate to Poor air quality during winter and spring, but enjoys Good to Satisfactory air quality during the summer monsoon months. The valley structure traps pollutants in the colder months, while heavy rains clear the air in the summer.

## Seasonal AQI Breakdown

- **Winter (November–February):** Most polluted period. Temperature inversion traps cold air, smoke, and dust near the ground. AQI typically 110–155 (Moderate to Poor).
- **Spring / Pre-Monsoon (March–May):** Dust storms and dry weather keep AQI at 90–125 (mostly Moderate, occasionally unhealthy for sensitive groups).
- **Monsoon (June–September):** Cleanest season — heavy rain washes dust and pollution out of the air. AQI drops to 35–80 (Good to Satisfactory).
- **Post-Monsoon (October):** Pollution slowly rebuilds as rains stop and temperatures drop; AQI rises back into the Moderate range.

## Seasonal Weather Patterns

Dehradun features a subtropical climate with four distinct seasons: hot summers, heavy monsoon rains, mild autumns, and chilly winters. The weather remains pleasant for most of the year compared to the plains of North India, though July and August see heavy downpours.

- **Winter (November–February):** Crisp, sunny days; cold nights. Daytime highs 21–26°C, nights drop to 6–11°C. January is typically the coldest month.
- **Spring / Summer (March–June):** Temperatures warm rapidly. May and June are hottest, with highs of 35–36°C.
- **Monsoon (July–September):** Heavy rainfall; July and August are wettest (21–23mm+ avg daily rainfall). Humidity is high, but temperatures cool to around 31°C.
- **Autumn (October):** Considered the best time to visit — rains clear completely, air is clean, days reach 30°C, nights cool to 16°C.
